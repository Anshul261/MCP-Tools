Ticket Time Analysis Dashboard

Overdue Requests and Total Requests Over Time

Total requests per month stayed fairly stable from January to April 2025, ranging from 1033 to 1238.

Overdue requests remain low compared to total requests, fluctuating slightly between 30 and 34 monthly.

First Response Overdue Requests Over Time

The count of first response overdue requests is higher than overdue requests, showing some variability month to month.

There was a peak of 215 overdue first responses in February 2025.

Average Resolution and First Response Times (Hours)

Average resolution times decreased significantly from about 91 hours in January to around 31 hours by April 2025.

Average first response times also improved, decreasing from over 19 hours to under 5 hours in that time period.
