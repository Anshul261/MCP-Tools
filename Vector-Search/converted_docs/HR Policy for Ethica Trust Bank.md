## HRPolicies for Ethica Trust Bank - A Shariah Compliant Islamic Bank in Sharjah

Preamble: This Human Resources Policy Manual outlines the principles, guidelines, and procedures governing the employment relationship between Ethica Trust Bank and its employees. All policies are designed to foster a fair, ethical, productive, and Shariahcompliant work environment, ensuring compliance with UAE Federal Labor Law, Sharjah regulations, and the principles of Islamic  nance and ethics derived from the Quran and Sunnah.

## I. Core Principles &amp; Shariah Compliance

## 1. Shariah Compliance Policy:

- o Objective: To ensure all HR policies, practices, and employee conduct align with Islamic Sharia principles.

## o Details:

- Sharia Supervisory Board (SSB) Oversight: The SSB will provide oversight and approval for all HR policies related to compensation, bene  ts, employee investment schemes, and ethical conduct. Any new  nancial scheme or signi  cant policy change impacting employee welfare or  nancial dealings must receive prior SSB approval.
- Prohibition of Riba: All employee loans, advances, or  nancial assistance o  ered by the bank shall strictly avoid Riba (interest). Approved alternatives include:
- Qard Hasan (Benevolent Loan): Interest-free loans granted for speci  c hardships (e.g., medical emergencies, education) or certain approved asset purchases (e.g., initial housing deposit). Repayment terms will be clearly stipulated and no additional charges will be levied. Approval process: Manager recommendation, Head of HR approval, and Finance Department disbursement. Maximum loan amount and repayment period will be de  ned annually based on bank's  nancial health and employee's tenure/salary.
- Pro fi t-Sharing Mechanisms: Exploration of Mudarabah (pro  t-sharing) or Musharakah (joint venture/equity participation) models for employee incentives or long-term savings schemes, subject to SSB approval.
- Prohibition of Haram Investments: Any employee-related investment schemes (e.g., provident funds, pension plans, longterm savings plans) managed or facilitated by the bank will only invest in Shariah-compliant assets and instruments, explicitly excluding industries such as alcohol, gambling, conventional interest-based  nance, pork products, and un-Islamic media. Funds will be managed by certi  ed Islamic fund managers.
- Ethical Conduct ( Akhlaq ): Employees are expected to demonstrate Akhlaq (good manners and morals) in all interactions. This includes honesty, trustworthiness ( Amanah ), justice ( Adl ), integrity, kindness, and mutual consultation ( Shura ). Any deviation from these principles may lead to disciplinary action.

## ▪ Religious Observances:

- Prayer Facilities: Dedicated, clean, and accessible prayer rooms (Musallah) for both male and female employees will be provided.
- Prayer Times: Flexible work adjustments to allow employees to perform their daily prayers without undue hardship. Managers are required to facilitate prayer breaks.
- Ramadan Working Hours: Adherence to reduced working hours during the holy month of Ramadan as per UAE Federal Labor Law.
- Zakat and Charitable Giving: While Zakat is a personal obligation, the bank may o  er a voluntary salary deduction scheme for Zakat contributions to approved charitable organizations. The bank itself will calculate and disburse its institutional Zakat in accordance with Shariah principles. Employees are encouraged to participate in the bank's CSR initiatives, which will be Shariah-compliant.
- Avoidance of Gharar and Maisir : Employee incentive structures, bonuses, and any  nancial schemes will be designed to avoid excessive uncertainty ( Gharar ) and gambling ( Maisir ). Performance metrics will be clear and measurable.

## 2. Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy:

- o Objective: To establish a framework for ethical behavior, professionalism, and integrity guided by Islamic values.
- o Details:
- Integrity and Honesty: Employees must act with utmost integrity, honesty, and transparency in all dealings, both internal and external. Falsehood, deception, or misrepresentation are strictly prohibited.
- Con fi dentiality ( Amanah ): Strict adherence to con  dentiality regarding bank, customer, and employee information. This extends to intellectual property, trade secrets, and non-public  nancial data. Unauthorized disclosure will lead to severe disciplinary action, including termination and legal action. This commitment continues post-employment.
- Con fl ict of Interest: Employees must avoid situations where their personal interests con  ict with the bank's interests. Any potential con  ict (e.g., signi  cant  nancial interest in a competitor, personal relationships with vendors) must be disclosed immediately to their Line Manager and the Head of HR.

## ▪ Gifts and Hospitality:

- Employees must not solicit, accept, or o  er gifts, entertainment, or hospitality that could be perceived as in  uencing business decisions or constituting Rishwah (bribery).
- Nominal, customary, and non-monetary gifts of small value (e.g., corporate branded items, refreshments during meetings) may be accepted if they do not create an obligation or appear to in  uence judgment. Any gift exceeding AED 500 in value must be declared to the Line Manager and HR, and may be donated to charity or returned.
- Anti-Bribery and Corruption: Zero tolerance for bribery, corruption, embezzlement, and any illicit practices. Employees must report any suspicious activity immediately.
- Fair Dealing ( Adl ): Treat all colleagues, customers, suppliers, and stakeholders with fairness, respect, and dignity, regardless of their background. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying are strictly prohibited.
- Shariah Board Directives: All employees must be aware of and comply with directives, fatwas, and guidelines issued by Ethica Trust Bank's Sharia Supervisory Board.
- Reporting Misconduct (Whistleblowing): A secure, con  dential, and non-retaliatory channel is established for reporting ethical violations, Shariah non-compliance, fraud, or other serious misconduct. Reports can be made to the Head of Internal Audit or a designated Ethics O  icer.

## V. Work-Life Balance and Employee Well-being

- o Objective: To de  ne working hours, attendance expectations, and procedures for managing punctuality and absences, in compliance with UAE labor law.

## o Details:

## 2. Leave Management Policy:

- o Objective: To provide clear guidelines on various types of leave available to employees, ensuring compliance with UAE labor law and accommodating religious obligations.

## o Details:

## ▪ Annual Leave:

- Entitlement: 30 calendar days of paid annual leave per year for employees who have completed one year of service. Pro-rata leave applies for partial years.
- Accrual: Leave accrues monthly.
- Application: Must be applied for at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR system/form.
- Approval: Line Manager approval required for up to 5 consecutive working days. Department Head approval for 6-15 consecutive working days. Head of HR approval for 16+ consecutive working days or extended periods.
- Carry-over: A maximum of 15 days of unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year. Excess accrued leave will be paid out as per UAE Labor Law.

## ▪ Sick Leave:

- Entitlement: As per UAE Labor Law: full pay for the  rst 15 days, half pay for the next 30 days, and unpaid for any subsequent period, provided sick leave is supported by a medical certi  cate.
- Application: Medical certi  cate from a registered medical practitioner required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Must be submitted to Line Manager and HR within 24 hours of returning to work.

## ▪ Maternity Leave:

- Entitlement: 60 calendar days of maternity leave:  rst 45 days at full pay, subsequent 15 days at half pay, for female employees who have completed at least one year of service. Pro-rata for less than one year of service.
- Additional Leave: Up to 100 unpaid days if the employee su  ers an illness as a result of pregnancy or delivery, requiring a medical certi  cate.
- Nursing Hours: 1 hour per day for nursing mothers for 6 months after returning to work.
- Approval: Head of HR.

## ▪ Paternity Leave:

- Entitlement: 5 working days of paid paternity leave within 6 months from the date of child's birth.
- Approval: Line Manager.

## ▪ Hajj/Umrah Leave:

- Entitlement: Up to 30 calendar days of unpaid leave (beyond annual leave entitlement) for Muslim employees to perform Hajj, once during their employment with Ethica Trust Bank, after completing at least 2 years of continuous service.
- Umrah: Employees may utilize annual leave for Umrah pilgrimage.
- Approval: Head of Department and Head of HR.
- Compassionate Leave:
- Entitlement: Up to 5 working days for the death of a spouse, up to 3 working days for the death of a parent, child, sibling, or grandparent.
- Approval: Line Manager.
- Study Leave: Unpaid leave may be granted for relevant higher education, subject to Line Manager and Head of HR approval, and operational requirements. Speci  c terms (duration, bond) to be agreed upon.
- Public Holidays: All o  icial UAE public holidays and Islamic holidays (as declared by the government) are observed as paid holidays.

## VI. Employee Relations and Grievance

## 1. Grievance Handling Policy:

- o Objective: To provide a fair, impartial, and prompt mechanism for employees to raise concerns and grievances.

## o Details:

- Stage 1 - Informal Resolution: Employee should  rst attempt to resolve the issue with their immediate Line Manager.
- Stage 2 - Formal Grievance: If unresolved, the employee may submit a formal written grievance to the Head of Department within 5 working days of the informal discussion. The Head of

Department will investigate and provide a written response within 7 working days.

- Stage 3 - HR Intervention: If still unresolved, the employee may escalate the grievance to the Head of HR within 5 working days of the Head of Department's response. The Head of HR will conduct a thorough investigation, potentially involving an HR committee, and provide a  nal written decision within 10 working days.
- Con fi dentiality: All grievances will be handled with strict con  dentiality to protect the privacy of all parties involved.
- Non-Retaliation: Employees raising genuine grievances in good faith will be protected from any form of retaliation.
- Emphasis on Mediation: Where appropriate and mutually agreed upon, mediation will be encouraged as a Shariah-compliant approach to con  ict resolution.

## 2. Disciplinary Policy:

- o Objective: To ensure fair and consistent application of disciplinary actions for misconduct or performance issues, guided by principles of justice and rehabilitation.

## o Details:

- Principles: Disciplinary action will be fair, consistent, proportional to the o  ense, and follow due process. The goal is to correct behavior and ensure compliance with bank policies and Shariah principles.

## ▪ Categorization of Misconduct:

- Minor Misconduct: (e.g., repeated tardiness, minor dress code violations, minor breaches of procedure).
- Serious Misconduct: (e.g., unauthorized disclosure of con  dential information, persistent insubordination, major policy breaches, gross negligence).
- Gross Misconduct: (e.g., fraud, theft, physical assault, severe Shariah non-compliance, serious breach of trust).
- Progressive Disciplinary Actions (for Minor/Serious Misconduct):
- Verbal Warning: Issued by Line Manager for minor o  enses. Documented internally.
- Written Warning (First): Issued by Line Manager with HR approval. Valid for 6 months.
- Written Warning (Final): Issued by Head of Department with HR approval. Valid for 12 months. May involve suspension without pay for up to 3 days as per UAE Labor Law.
- Termination: For repeated serious misconduct or failure to improve after  nal warning, in accordance with UAE Labor Law.
- Investigation Procedure: For serious misconduct, a formal investigation will be conducted by HR, potentially involving a disciplinary committee. The employee will be given the opportunity to present their side of the story.
- Right to Be Heard and Appeal: Employees have the right to be informed of the allegations, present their defense, and appeal a disciplinary decision to the Head of HR (for decisions made by Line Manager/Dept Head) or to the CEO (for decisions made by Head of HR/Disciplinary Committee) within 5 working days of receiving the decision.
- Immediate Termination (for Gross Misconduct): As per UAE Labor Law, certain acts of gross misconduct (e.g., theft, assault, disclosure of secrets causing damage) may result in immediate termination without notice or end-of-service bene  ts. Such cases will undergo thorough investigation and veri  cation.

## 3. Termination of Employment Policy:

- o Objective: To outline the procedures for voluntary and involuntary termination of employment, ensuring compliance with UAE labor law and fair practices.

## o Details:

- Resignation: Employees wishing to resign must submit a written resignation letter to their Line Manager and HR, adhering to the notice period stipulated in their employment contract (typically 3090 calendar days).
- Termination by Bank (with notice): For reasons such as redundancy, poor performance (after PIP), or continued serious misconduct, the bank may terminate employment with written notice as per the employment contract and UAE Labor Law (typically 30-90 calendar days for unlimited contracts).
- Termination without Notice (Gross Misconduct): As per UAE Labor Law Article 120, speci  c instances of gross misconduct may lead to immediate termination without notice or gratuity. This is applied only after a thorough investigation and due process.
- End-of-Service Bene fi ts (Gratuity): Calculated and paid strictly as per UAE Federal Labor Law (pro-rata for  rst 5 years, then full for subsequent years), upon completion of service, provided the termination is not for gross misconduct as per Article 120.
- Exit Interview: All departing employees will be o  ered an exit interview with HR to provide feedback on their employment experience.
- Handover Process: A clear and comprehensive handover of responsibilities, documents, and bank property is mandatory for all departing employees.
